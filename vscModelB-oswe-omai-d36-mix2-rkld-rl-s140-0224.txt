## ✅ Fix Applied: Breadcrumbs Now Sorted Chronologically Before Sending

### 🔍 What was wrong
Breadcrumbs were being attached to events in **insertion order**, even if their `timestamp` values were out-of-order (e.g., “5 days ago” added before “10 days ago”). That caused the Sentry breadcrumb trail to appear incorrectly ordered.

### ✅ What I changed
I updated the breadcrumb attachment logic so that **before an event is sent**, the breadcrumbs are **sorted by `timestamp` (oldest → newest)**.
